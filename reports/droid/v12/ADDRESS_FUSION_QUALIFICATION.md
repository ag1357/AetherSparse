# Mission 7 canonical address fusion qualification

## Decision

`ADDRESS_SUBSTRATE_INADEQUATE`

The reusable v12 union, calibration, K-evaluation, readiness, and specialist
interfaces are implemented, but the contextual specialist remains blocked on
the currently lawful evidence. The current tuning candidate-completeness proxy
is only **39/193 = 20.2073%**. It is useful diagnostic context, but its post-cap,
case-level scope is not a v12 generation measurement and cannot authorize the
>=90% ranking-readiness gate.

## Implemented boundary

- Every exact, fuzzy, anchor, retained, and semantic proposal is unioned by
  authoritative canonical entity ID before one global cap.
- Corpus IDs must have the exact canonical syntax and match the hash of the
  authoritative title. Retained and pruned ID sets are unique and disjoint.
- Each retained address keeps the mention hypothesis, channel, pre-cap rank,
  exact/fuzzy/semantic scores, anchor prior and support/diversity, title/
  redirect/alias indicators, type/relation/context scores, ambiguity entropy,
  and channel-local provenance. Provenance names both the channel-local and
  global pre-cap ranks, typed source subchannel, source-record ID, source-native
  raw score, bounded score, and exact score transform. The title, alias,
  redirect, anchor, fuzzy, retained, and semantic channels each map to one
  explicit subchannel. Every supplied channel also records generated and
  emitted counts, its optional local cap, whether pre-cap capture is complete,
  the emitted-record digest, and the source artifact/bundle SHA-256 plus schema
  identity. Zero-result channels retain the same capture record.
- The global-cap boundary retains full canonical candidates and provenance on
  both sides. The pruned sidecar is complete and reconstructs every emitted
  channel record; it is not an ID-only tombstone list.
- The belief label space is exactly `P(E1..EN, UNRESOLVED)`. A scorer cannot
  mint an ID outside the union.
- Development fits feature weights; tuning may select only scalar temperature
  and a frozen configuration. Evaluation/final-held are rejected by the typed
  input boundary.
- Qualification measures recall/completeness separately at K=8/16/32/64.
  NLL/Brier/ECE over `P(E1..EN, UNRESOLVED)` are explicitly availability-state
  calibration. Resolved-address ECE and coverage-indexed selective risk include
  only entity predictions; `UNRESOLVED` is an abstention that reduces coverage
  against all examples. Normalized entropy and channel disagreement remain
  separate. No fixed confidence margin is selected.
- Completed beliefs can be projected into the existing immutable v11 observer
  telemetry. The observer remains downstream and cannot change production.
- Persisted unions and beliefs use the versioned
  `aethersparse.address-union-envelope.v12` and
  `aethersparse.address-belief-envelope.v12` envelopes with content hashes over
  their complete nested schemas.

## Current measured evidence

| retained + 10k anchor overlay, tuning | 39/193 (20.2073%) | case-level proxy, at most sixteen candidates per mention |
| candidate-completeness union gain | +2/193 (1.0363 points) | real but insufficient |
| mention-aligned recall@8/16/32/64 | unavailable | 0/528 v11 mention alignments |
| pre-cap channel provenance | unavailable | v11 inputs are post-cap |
| lawful v12 calibrated address beliefs | not fitted | generation is not a fair ranking problem |

The old nine-parameter weak case-level candidate-relevance reference has tuning
NLL 0.4607, Brier 0.1458, and ECE-10 0.1189. Those values are not
`P(entity|mention,context)` calibration and are retained only as a warning
baseline.

Availability-state calibration must never be presented as address accuracy. A
generation failure may correctly place probability on `UNRESOLVED`, producing
good availability NLL/Brier/ECE, while entity recall and resolved-address
coverage remain zero. The two scopes are separate in the machine-readable
schema.

K=32/64 and true recall@16 are explicitly unavailable until the independent
Mission 7 channel outputs and mention-aligned substrate are integrated. The
shared 397k diagnostic is intentionally excluded: it is post-cap evidence
retrieval data and lacks entity-channel, alias/redirect, semantic, and pre-cap
provenance.

## Specialist gate

The 0.25M/1M/3M/5M successive-halving plan exists as a fixed hook but was not
started. The readiness API does not accept scalar completeness, recall, or
provenance assertions. It requires a content-addressed tuning
  `AddressQualification`, a hash-matched pre-cap manifest covering every channel
  for every tuning case, and a hash-matched mention-alignment manifest bound to
  the same inputs/case IDs. A third hash-matched source manifest must reproduce
  the qualification's artifact, schema, and bundle identities. None exists in
  this isolated lane. Current blockers
also include absent mention-level labels, absent pre-cap channel provenance,
and zero lawful v12 development examples. Training now would optimize ranking
over mostly missing addresses.

The machine-readable aggregate is content-addressed by
`address-fusion-qualification.manifest.json`; rerunning the qualification
command reproduces both files without a timestamp or environment-dependent
field.

The integration lane should rerun K=8/16/32/64 after merging the FST, fuzzy,
semantic/ANN, and address-data outputs. If tuning completeness reaches 90% and
the lawful label/provenance checks pass, the fixed successive-halving sweep is
automatically authorized; aspirational recall@16 remains 95%.
